Private documents in.
Training data you can trace out.

Chaos-to-Model turns a folder of PDFs, scans, ebooks and office files into training data on one Mac. Every row keeps its source file, its page and the box on that page, so a generated answer can be traced to the passage it came from.

Results

Benchmarked against Unsloth Data Recipes and NeMo Curator

Unsloth ran its PDF Document QA recipe at a pinned Studio commit; NeMo Curator ran its Nemotron-CC recipes at v1.4.0. Every change from the upstream recipes is recorded in the full report.

+4.8 ptsexact-match answers over NeMo Curator, 95% interval +0.2 to +9.4, from 377 training rows against 1,396
Pre-registeredevaluation protocol committed before any run, its hash recorded in the run manifest
5 of 6 foldsscored so far; the rest are reported when they finish, whatever they show

Where c2m does not lead

  • On the pre-registered primary score, "contains the answer", no tuned set separates from another, and the untuned student scores highest because it answers at length.
  • NeMo Curator's students give the gold answer the highest likelihood, clearly ahead of c2m.
  • c2m's set includes short-answer rows that match the evaluation's reply format. An earlier single split, run before those rows existed, favoured Unsloth on exact match.
  • Character and table counts describe output, not accuracy. NeMo Curator ran without its GPU parser; Unsloth ran with its default OCR language and page limit.
